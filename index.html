Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the adoption process take?

Most adoptions are completed within one to two weeks, including an application review, a meet-and-greet, and a home check.

Can I foster before I adopt?

Yes — our foster-to-adopt program lets you bring a pet home for a trial period before finalizing the adoption.

What does the adoption fee cover?

Spay/neuter surgery, vaccinations, microchipping, and an initial health check are all included in the adoption fee.
